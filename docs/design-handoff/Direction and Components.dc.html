Design direction · v2

Ivory, Oxford navy & a crest. The school’s own register.

Made for Egypt’s private and language schools: ivory grounds, Oxford navy for action, a burgundy-and-gold crest for identity, and serif headings that read like a school prospectus. Teachers are Ms. and Mr.; parents are addressed with respect and reassured first. Status colours still carry a shape and a word. راقٍ، هادئ، يمكن الوثوق به.

3 · Type

Headings: Source Serif 4 + Noto Naskh Arabic, the prospectus voice. UI and data: IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Sans Arabic, designed as one family so mixed lines sit on the same rhythm. Arabic runs one step larger at the same token (x-height parity) and +0.15 line-height. Times always Latin, tabular figures. Plex Mono only for codes and device addresses.

7 · Rules of the room

Live, never jumpy. Rows don't re-sort mid-lesson once a teacher has touched one; values crossfade in place; "Updated just now" is the only signal. Focus is never moved by a refresh.

Red is information. Absent uses a clear brick red on a pale tint, never a filled red row. The word always appears beside it.

RTL by mirroring, not rewriting. Layouts use logical sides; times stay Latin (07:42) and read LTR inside Arabic sentences.
