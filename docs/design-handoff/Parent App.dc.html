Parent app · Nile Valley Language School

360 × 760 phone. Same ivory-and-navy tokens and crest as the staff portal; dark mode swaps navy for gold. Status pills keep glyph + word. Parents activate their account with the email registered at Student Affairs, then verify their mobile on WhatsApp. Messages reassure first: “arrived safely”, “Student Affairs is following up”.
